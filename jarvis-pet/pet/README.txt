Put your own pet's pictures in this folder to replace the built-in robot.

Easiest: drop in ONE picture (PNG or GIF). It becomes your pet.

For more expression, name pictures after what the pet is doing:

  idle.png     standing around (required if you use names)
  walk.gif     walking (it gets flipped automatically when walking left)
  talk.gif     answering you
  listen.png   listening to the microphone
  think.gif    waiting for Claude to answer
  sleep.png    napping
  happy.png  excited.png  love.png  sad.png  dance.gif   moods Claude can show

Any picture that's missing falls back to idle. Animated GIFs play.
PNGs with a see-through background look best.
Restart the pet after adding pictures.
