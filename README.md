# Flick Range

A browser aim trainer. Open `index.html` in a desktop browser and click a drill. The game locks your mouse; press Esc to pause.

## Drills

- **Gridshot**: three targets on a grid; clear them fast.
- **Spidershot**: one target jumps between the center and the edges.
- **Microshot**: tiny targets in random spots.
- **Head Level**: dummies at different ranges. Only headshots kill, and body shots break your combo.
- **Peek Duel**: targets swing out from behind pillars and duck back. The window gets shorter as the drill goes on.
- **Strafe Track**: keep your crosshair on a strafing target.
- **Reaction**: 10 rounds of "click when it appears". Clicking early adds 30 ms.

## Daily challenge

Each day picks one drill with a fixed random seed, so everyone gets the same targets that day. Play it on back-to-back days to build a streak.

## Ranks, stats and combos

- Every drill gives a rank from Bronze to Grandmaster, and the results screen tells you the score for the next rank.
- The last 60 runs of each drill are saved in your browser. "Your stats" on the menu shows graphs and recent averages.
- Hitting targets in a row builds a combo that adds bonus points. Missing resets it.

## Crosshair

Under "Crosshair" you can change color, line length, thickness, gap, outline and center dot, or pick a preset. Each crosshair has a code like `fr1-3fd8c9-5-2-3-10` that you can copy and share; paste someone else's code and press Load to use theirs.

## Sensitivity

Sensitivity uses Valorant's scale (0.07° per mouse count at sens 1). Set it with the slider on the menu or pause screen, or press [ and ] while playing. Settings also use Valorant's horizontal FOV and show your eDPI and cm per 360°.
